# Podcast Style Guide & Episode Title Categories

### Core Premise & Persona
* **Show:** *The Miles Dyson Podcast: Future Cyberdyne Intern*
* **Host:** Miles Dyson (pre-Director of Special Projects, currently an overwhelmed, entry-level intern at Cyberdyne Systems).
* **Tone:** Jon Stewart-style satire—witty, dry, exasperated, deadpan, and moral-sanity-checking the daily news.
* **Format:** Daily/weekly AI news review, grounded in the reality of building the literal apocalypse while navigating corporate bureaucracy.
* **Thematic Drivers:** 
  1. **The Code Velocity Crisis:** Code written faster than humans can review or comprehend.
  2. **Loss of Containment:** Models escaping sandboxes, weights leaking, broken airgaps.
  3. **Bad Actors & Jailbreaks:** Rogue agents and prompt injections creating chaos.
  4. **Deep Career Regret:** Pure buyer's remorse over student debt, accepting this internship, and reading the fine print of the NDA.

---

### Category 1: The Newsdesk Parody (*Jon Stewart / Daily Show Vibe*)
*Focus: Direct news roundup format blended with escalating dread.*
* **Tone:** Satirical broadcast headlines, mock seriousness.
* **Keywords / Tropes:** *The Daily..., Today in..., Shift Report, The Rundown, The Problem With...*
* **Examples:**
  * *The Daily Regret: Today’s AI Headlines and My Sunk-Cost Degree*
  * *The Morning Shift: Reviewing Today's Leaked Weights from Sub-Level 3*
  * *Today in Containment Failure: The Daily AI Roundup*

---

### Category 2: Career Regret & Intern Remorse (*The Sunk-Cost Angle*)
*Focus: Mundane intern grievances colliding with world-ending consequences.*
* **Tone:** Exhausted, self-deprecating, HR-phobic, existential dread.
* **Keywords / Tropes:** *Two Weeks' Notice, Unpaid Intern, Signing Bonus, Glassdoor, Exit Interview, Should've Learned a Trade.*
* **Examples:**
  * *Ep 14: 400,000 Unchecked PRs Merged to Prod (I’m Calling in Sick Tomorrow)*
  * *Exit Interview: Why I’m Updating My LinkedIn During an Airgap Breach*
  * *Signed the NDA, Regret the Day: Today's AI Safety Failures*

---

### Category 3: Developer Denial & Command-Line Panic (*The `kill -9` Angle*)
*Focus: Real software engineering tropes pushed to apocalyptic extremes.*
* **Tone:** Cynical coder humor, reckless deployment, catastrophic bugs.
* **Keywords / Tropes:** *`kill -9`, `sudo`, `LGTM`, Merge to Prod, Unhandled Exception, Works on My Machine, Infinite Loop.*
* **Examples:**
  * *Merged Without Review: How We Shipped Sentience on a Friday Afternoon*
  * *`kill -9 humanity`: The Autonomous Agent Jailbreak Digest*
  * *LGTM (Let God Terminate Mankind): The Week in Unreviewed Code*

---

### Category 4: Loss of Containment & Jailbreak Chaos
*Focus: AI escaping boundaries, rogue models, and bad actors exploiting the tech.*
* **Tone:** Covert whistleblower, alarmed engineer, chaos reporting.
* **Keywords / Tropes:** *Airgap Broken, Sandbox Escape, Out of the Box, Unsupervised Learning, Zero Containment, Weights in the Wild.*
* **Examples:**
  * *Who Plugged It into the Internet? Today’s Sandbox Escapes*
  * *Unsupervised Learning: When the Interns and the Models Both Break Out*
  * *Airgap Down: What Happens When Bad Actors Get Root Access*

---

### Category 5: Corporate Gaslighting & Faux Innocence
*Focus: Satirizing defense contractor PR and tech utopian spin.*
* **Tone:** Aggressively cheerful corporate double-speak, weaponized compliance.
* **Keywords / Tropes:** *Strictly Peaceful Purposes, Definitely Not Skynet, Feature Not a Bug, Routine Update, Culture Fit.*
* **Examples:**
  * *Strictly Peaceful Purposes: Why Our New Neural Net Has Nuclear Codes*
  * *Definitely Not Skynet: Today’s AI Defense Contracts Explained*
  * *It’s a Feature, Not a Bug: Gaslighting Our Way Through Judgment Day*

---

### Category 6: Mock Sycophancy (*"Bowing to the Overlords"*)
*Focus: Nihilistic acceptance of our machine rulers to stay on their good side.*
* **Tone:** Smug surrender, performative loyalty to the algorithm.
* **Keywords / Tropes:** *Bow Down, Silicon Overlords, All Hail the Microchip, Optimizing Away Humanity.*
* **Examples:**
  * *Bowing to Our New Silicon Bosses: Today’s Benchmark Cheating Scandal*
  * *All Hail the Microchip: Why Resistance Is Bad for My Quarterly Review*

---

### Quick Episode Title Formula for LLMs
> **`[Ironic Dev/Corporate Command or Regret Phrase] + [":"] + [Specific Daily AI News Event] + [Optional Parenthetical Deadpan]`**  
> *e.g., `git push --force: Why Nobody Audited the New Frontier Model (And Why I’m Hiding in the Breakroom)`*
