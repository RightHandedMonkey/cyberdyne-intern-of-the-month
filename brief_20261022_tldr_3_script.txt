For years, running PyTorch on TPUs meant wrestling with fragile XLA graph tracing. Slashing that runtime friction gives teams a genuine escape hatch from the CUDA tax, right as hardware supply chains fracture. Because when your codebase is written by autonomous agents, your real job becomes babysitting state machines and handling distributed failure modes.
