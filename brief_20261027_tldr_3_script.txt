It is a fascinating juxtaposition against developer tooling, where platforms like Cursor are now granting agents direct control over local operating system surfaces and application simulators. When infrastructure Capex requires structured financial instruments typically reserved for deep-sea oil refineries, it confirms that raw compute demand has entirely broken away from traditional venture math. Everyone is mortgaging balance sheets just to ensure they are not caught on the wrong side of the silicon allocation curve when next-generation clusters spin up.

As persistent agents transition from text prediction to credentialed execution, our entire architectural surface is shifting toward kernel-level sandboxing and formal authorization protocols.
